OrbitFlip

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OrbitFlip ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Do Not Collect

OrbitFlip does not require an account, and we do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, or physical address. We do not maintain servers that hold personal data about you.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is saved locally on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your scores and personal best records for each game mode
  • Game mode selection and unlocked progression
  • Language preference and app settings (music, sound effects, haptics)
  • Purchase status for premium features

This data is removed when you delete the app, except the progress you keep in iCloud (see below).

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, OrbitFlip stores a copy of your best scores and unlocked progression in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage), so a new device or a reinstall can restore them. This data goes to Apple's iCloud under your Apple ID; we cannot see or access it. Settings, language, onboarding state and purchases are not synced. You can turn off iCloud for OrbitFlip in iOS Settings › Apple ID › iCloud. Resetting progress in the app also clears the iCloud copy.

Third-Party Services

No Advertising

OrbitFlip does not show advertisements and does not include any advertising or analytics SDK. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Apple Game Center

If you enable Game Center, your scores may be submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center profile. This is managed entirely by Apple. Game Center is optional and can be disabled in your device settings.

In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details. Apple handles all transaction and billing information according to its own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

OrbitFlip includes a Kids Mode designed for younger players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app requires no account or personal data to play.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. The only data leaving your device goes to the third-party services listed above, under their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions
  • Disable Game Center in iOS Settings
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data, and turn off iCloud for OrbitFlip (or reset progress in the app) to remove the iCloud copy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
